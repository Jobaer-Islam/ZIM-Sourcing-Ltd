We review your specification, check feasibility with suitable units and come back with an indicative costing. Nothing is chargeable at this point.

How ordering works

Nine defined stages from enquiry to delivery.

Each stage has a named owner, a deliverable and a date. Nothing advances until the previous stage is approved in writing.

STEP 01

Enquiry & costing

Send styles, quantities and target price. We come back with an indicative costing.

STEP 02

Development

Proto sample produced with fabric and trim options against your tech pack.

STEP 03

Fit & approval

Fit samples, lab dips, strike-offs and size sets approved before bulk booking.

STEP 04

Order confirmation

PO issued, factory allocated, TNA calendar published and materials booked.

STEP 05

PP meeting

Pre-production sample approved and the PP meeting held on the sewing floor.

STEP 06

Bulk production

Inline QC through the sewing run, with regular photo and progress reporting.

STEP 07

Final inspection

Final random inspection with a written report and photographs before ex-factory.

STEP 08

Shipment

Booking, stuffing, and full document set to your bank and forwarder.

STEP 09

After delivery

Delivery review, defect analysis and carry-forward notes for the next season.

Compliance

Every factory is verified before your order is placed.

Before allocation we check each factory for a valid social audit, current structural, fire and electrical safety certification, and a documented chemical and effluent policy. Lapsed certification means no order.

  • Social. No child or forced labour, legal wages, controlled overtime, freedom of association and a working grievance channel.
  • Safety. RSC-aligned structural, fire and electrical remediation with evidence of closed corrective actions.
  • Environment. ETP operation, chemical inventories to ZDHC principles, and water and energy reduction targets.
