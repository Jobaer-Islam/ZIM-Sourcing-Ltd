Products & services

Products and services.

Six categories, one sourcing team. Each section below sets out the constructions, fabrics and finishes we run regularly. Quantities and timing are confirmed per programme.

Request a quotation See how ordering works
Category 01

Knitwear

Our largest category, and the strongest part of the Bangladesh base. Most knit programmes run through vertically integrated units with knitting, dyeing, cutting and sewing on one site, which shortens lead times and holds shade consistency.

Products
T-shirts, polo shirts, hoodies, sweatshirts, joggers, leggings, tank tops, jersey dresses, pyjama and lounge sets, base layers, activewear
Constructions
Single jersey, pique, interlock, 1×1 and 2×2 rib, French terry, loopback, brushed fleece, waffle, slub, melange
Fibres
Combed and carded cotton, organic cotton (GOTS), BCI cotton, cotton/elastane, polyester, recycled polyester (GRS), viscose, modal, TENCEL™, cotton/poly blends
Weights
Light jersey through to heavy brushed fleece
Decoration
Screen, pigment, discharge, water-based, puff, HD and foil print; all-over print; embroidery; appliqué; heat transfer; woven and printed labels

Category 02

Woven

Structured product in which pattern engineering and fit consistency are decisive. Our woven units operate dedicated sample rooms and retain graded blocks between seasons, so repeat orders match the original approved fit.

Products
Formal and casual shirts, blouses, dresses, skirts, trousers, chinos, shorts, cargos, jumpsuits, light outerwear, overshirts
Constructions
Poplin, twill, oxford, chambray, dobby, seersucker, canvas, ripstop, corduroy, flannel, linen weave, satin
Fibres
Cotton, cotton/elastane, linen and linen blends, viscose, rayon, polyester, poly/cotton, TENCEL™, recycled polyester (GRS)
Finishes
Peach, enzyme, easy-care, wrinkle-resistant, water-repellent, garment dye, garment wash, yarn-dyed checks and stripes
Detailing
Engineered plackets, matched checks, taped seams, felled seams, embroidery, pintucks, pleating, printed and woven trims
Category 03

Sweater

Flat-knit production from heavy 3GG constructions to fine 14GG merino. Sweater lead times are frequently underestimated, as yarn dyeing and linking are time-intensive. We plan sweater calendars accordingly.

Products
Crew and V-neck pullovers, cardigans, zip-throughs, roll and funnel necks, vests, knitted dresses, scarves, beanies, knitted accessories
Gauges
3GG, 5GG, 7GG, 9GG, 12GG, 14GG, computerised flat-knit and semi-automatic machines
Techniques
Jacquard, intarsia, cable, links-links, pointelle, rib structures, fully fashioned panels, hand linking, tipping and contrast trims
Yarns
Acrylic, cotton, cotton/acrylic, wool, lambswool, merino, mohair blends, alpaca blends, chenille, recycled polyester and recycled acrylic
Note
Yarn-dyed and multi-colour jacquard programmes take longer to plan. Book yarn early for autumn and winter ranges.

Category 04

Denim & Bottoms

Wash development is central to denim. We develop recipes with partner laundries operating laser, ozone and enzyme processes, which reduces water and pumice consumption and provides a documented, repeatable recipe for reorders.

Products
Five-pocket jeans, jeggings, wide-leg and straight fits, denim jackets and shirts, skirts, shorts, dungarees, non-denim bottoms
Fabrics
Rigid denim, stretch and super-stretch, comfort stretch, black and coated denim across the usual weight range, recycled cotton denim, hemp blends
Wash & finish
Rinse, stone, enzyme, bleach, tint, over-dye, acid, laser whisker and 3D crease, ozone fading, hand sand, destruction and repair, resin
Sustainability
Laser and ozone finishing to reduce water and chemistry; wash recipes documented and reproducible across reorders
Detailing
Custom rivets, shank buttons, leather and jacron patches, chain-stitch hems, contrast bar-tacks, embroidery
Category 05

Uniforms & Workwear

Uniform programmes require consistent sizing across multiple years, fabric suitable for industrial laundering, and dependable replenishment. We retain approved size sets and fabric lots between drops so later reorders match the original delivery.

Sectors
Corporate and office, hotel and hospitality, restaurant and kitchen, healthcare and medical scrubs, industrial and manufacturing, school, security, aviation, retail
Products
Shirts and blouses, polos, trousers, tunics, scrubs, chef wear, aprons, coveralls, boiler suits, hi-vis vests, caps and headwear and jackets, lab coats, waistcoats
Fabrics
Poly/cotton 65/35 and 80/20, twill, gabardine, ripstop, stretch performance, antimicrobial and soil-release finishes, hi-vis fluorescent with retro-reflective tape
Programme features
Locked size specs, retained fabric lots, name and logo personalisation, size-run packing, drop-ship by location, annual replenishment calendars
Standards
EN ISO 20471 hi-vis and EN ISO 13688 general requirements available on request through certified partner units

Category 06

Kidswear

Children’s apparel is subject to the strictest safety regulations in the industry. Every kidswear programme is developed against the destination market’s requirements from the outset, rather than verified only at final inspection.

Range

Newborn and infant bodysuits, sleepsuits, toddler sets, boys’ and girls’ tops and bottoms, dresses, outerwear, sleepwear, school uniform.

Safety standards

EN 14682 cords and drawstrings, EN 71-3 migration limits, CPSIA lead and phthalates, 16 CFR 1615/1616 sleepwear flammability, nickel release, small-parts pull testing.

Testing

Third-party testing through SGS, Intertek, Bureau Veritas or TUV, arranged and tracked by us, with reports issued before shipment.

Full-service sourcing

Services included in our scope.

All covered by our buying commission, not billed separately.

Factory sourcing & allocation

Matching your product, volume, price point and compliance requirements to the right unit, and re-allocating if a factory’s performance slips.

Design & product development

Tech pack build from sketches or samples, pattern making, graded size sets, fit correction and counter-sample development.

Fabric & trim sourcing

Mill sourcing local and imported, lab dips, hand looms, strike-offs, trim cards, and full material bookings against your approved standards.

Costing & negotiation

Open-book cost sheets showing fabric, trims, CM, wash, testing and freight, so you can see exactly where a price comes from.

Merchandising & TNA

A dated time-and-action calendar per order, regular written updates, and early escalation the moment a milestone is at risk.

Quality assurance

Fabric inspection on receipt, inline quality checks through the sewing run, and a final random inspection against recognised sampling standards before shipment.

Compliance verification

Audit certificate checks before allocation, corrective action follow-up, and facilitation of your own or third-party audits.

Packing & labelling

Solid, ratio and assortment packing, barcode and price ticketing, GS1 carton marking, hangers and retail-ready presentation.

Shipping & documentation

Freight booking or nominated-forwarder coordination, container stuffing supervision, and the complete export document set.

Mixed constructions

Styles that span more than one category.

Many styles combine constructions, such as a jersey dress with a woven yoke or a workwear polo with performance finishing. Send a sketch or reference sample and we will identify the appropriate manufacturing unit.
