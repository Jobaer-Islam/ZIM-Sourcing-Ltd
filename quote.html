Request a quote

Send your style. Get an indicative FOB quotation.

A complete tech pack gives the most accurate costing, but you do not need everything ready to contact us. Send the information you have, including sketches, photographs, fabric details or reference samples, and our merchandising team will review it.

Quotation review

Reviewing your enquiry and preparing an indicative costing is free of charge.

NDA available

A non-disclosure agreement can be signed before you send confidential designs or technical information.

Sampling and courier

Physical samples, courier, laboratory testing and external inspection are quoted and agreed separately before any cost is incurred.

What happens after you submit

  1. We confirm receipt by email.
  2. We review the specification and come back on anything unclear.
  3. We identify suitable production options for the product and quantity.
  4. You receive an indicative costing and a proposed development calendar.
  5. Sampling is discussed only if you decide to continue.
